Guideline for the Optimal Blood Ordering for Elective Surgery
Mi-Jeung Gwak, M.D., Pyung-Hwan Park, M.D. and Dae-won Kim, M.D.*
Department of Anesthesiology and Clinical Pathology," Asan Medical Center,
Ulsan University, College of Medicine
In this study, the authors would like to establish the guideline for effective utilization of
blood and blood components in various elective surgeries.
We investigated the amount of tansfused blood and blood components units, C/T ratio
(crossmatching to transfusion ratio), disused rate of blood ordered in 861 elective surgery cases
during the period from October 1990 to September 1991.
overall mean units of transfusional units per patient were 5.3 units and overall C/T ratio
was 1.14. Of all the transfusional units the ratio of whole blood was 51.5%, packed red blood cell
was 7%, fresh frozen plasma was 41% respectively.
From this study, the guideline for the optimal blood ordering for each elective surgery was
made.
Key Words: Blood ordering, C/T ratio, Elective surgery
