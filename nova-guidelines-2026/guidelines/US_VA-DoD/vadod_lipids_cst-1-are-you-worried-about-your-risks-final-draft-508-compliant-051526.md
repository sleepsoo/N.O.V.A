## ARE YOU WORRIED ABOUT YOUR RISK

### OF HEART ATTACK OR STROKE?

#### **Risk factors for heart attack or stroke:**

- Tobacco Use

- High Blood Pressure

- Diabetes

- High Cholesterol

##### **_Know Your Risk!_**

- Family History

- Prior Heart Disease

- Age

- **Talk with your healthcare provider about your 10-year heart and stroke risk (PREVENT score)**

- **Ask about additional testing like a coronary calcium scan**

- For more information, see the VA/DoD Clinical Practice Guideline: Management of Dyslipidemia

https://www.healthquality.va.gov/guidelines/CD/lipids/

# WHAT CAN I DO TO REDUCE MY RISK?

Quit Smoking

Be More Physically Active

Achieve a Healthy Weight

KNOW YOUR RISK

AND LOWER IT

Eat a Heart-Healthy Diet

Take CholesterolLowering Medication if Recommended

Control Blood Pressure
