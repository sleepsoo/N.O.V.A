## **Antipsychotics**

Favor minimal-weight-impact antidepressants.

No antipsychotic is associated with weight loss. Consider agents with minimal weight gain when possible.

## **Heart Medications**

## **Antiretrovirals**

Consider calcium channel blockers, angiotensin receptor blockers, angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors, and thiazide or loop diuretics, as indicated. Other antihypertensive classes may be options depending on indication. (e.g., angina, heart failure, HTN, migraine).

Other ARVs are typically weight-neutral. Prioritize viremia control.

### **Anti-Allergy Agents**

Depending on symptoms, consider alternatives such as: ipratropium nasal spray, decongestants, inhalers, and/or nonpharmacologic measures (e.g., nasal irrigation)
