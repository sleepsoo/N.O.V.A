## **Sidebar 1: Initial Individualized Treatment Plan**

Discuss an individualized wellness and self-management program, which can include:

- Provide education on self-management of OA and treatment goals (see **Recommendation 4** )

- Increase physical activity (e.g., walking, cycling) (see **Recommendation 4** )

- Engage in regular self-directed exercise if appropriate (e.g., selfstrengthening, yoga, tai chi) (see **Recommendation 31** )

- Recommend weight reduction for patients who are overweight or obese: refer to the current VA/DOD CPG for the Management of Adult Overweight and Obesity* (see **Recommendations 2 and 3** )

- Offer referral for physical therapy and bracing if indicated** (see **Recommendations 5-7** )

Pharmacotherapy which can include one or more of the following:

- Topical agents for OA of the knee (e.g., NSAIDs, capsaicin) (see **Recommendations 10 and 11** )

 - ♦ Topical agents for OA of the knee (e.g., NSAIDs or capsaicin)

 - ♦ Acetaminophen† (see **Recommendation 12** )

 - ♦ NSAIDs including COX-2 inhibitors‡ (see **Recommendation 12** )

## **Sidebar 2: Next Steps in Care**

- Offer a different combination of initial treatments (see **Sidebar 1** )

- In select patients, consider adding duloxetine as an alternative or adjunct to initial treatments (see **Recommendation 13** )

## **Sidebar 3: Consider Intra-Articular Injections**

- CSI for hip and knee OA* (see **Recommendations 15 and 16** )

*CSI should be avoided for the three months preceding joint replacement surgery; CSI for the hip should be image-guided
