# **Lipid Management for Cardiovascular Disease Risk Reduction**

**Management Algorithm***

*Values for estimated risk are based on the PREVENT risk assessment tool.

## **Sidebar 6: For Statin Intolerance****

• Increase physical activity (aerobic and resistance exercise) that maximizes what the patient is willing and able to achieve

- MI or ACS

 1. Washout period (e.g., 1 month) followed by the same or a different statin; continue other lipid-lowering therapy

- CABG/PCI

• The stated goals of minutes per week are 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity OR 75 minutes of vigorous-intensity physical activity OR an equivalent combination.

- Stable CAD

- • CVA/TIA due to atherosclerosis

 2. Lower dose or nondaily dosing (e.g., every other day or 2-3 days per week) of statin (see **Recommendation 18** )

 3. Consider initiating bempedoic acid, ezetimibe, fibrates, or PCSK9 mAb inhibitors in patients unable to take a statin (see **Recommendation 19** )

- PAD

• Does **not** include asymptomatic atherosclerosis on imaging (e.g., CCTA, CAC, catheterization)

- Choose a healthy dietary pattern (e.g., Mediterranean diet)

- • Sleep 7-8 hours/night • Socialize: forge and embrace social connections • Quit using tobacco and nicotine • Minimize alcohol consumption • Manage stress • Address overweight and obesity

## **Sidebar 8: Elevated Triglycerides for Secondary CVD Prevention**

Consider secondary causes of elevated triglycerides† If triglycerides are persistently elevated (≥150 mg/dL) despite maximally tolerated statin, then consider icosapent ethyl 2 g BID (see **Recommendation 16** ) Modify diet
