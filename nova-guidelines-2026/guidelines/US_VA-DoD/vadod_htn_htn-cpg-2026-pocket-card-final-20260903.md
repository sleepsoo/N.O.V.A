- Evaluate for interfering substances (e.g., nutritional and other supplements including teas [such as licorice root], prescription and/or over-the-counter medications, NSAIDs, alcohol, recreational drugs)

*Greater reduction seen in patients with a higher baseline BP.

**Adding an agent from a different pharmaceutical class offers significantly greater blood pressure reduction and often better tolerability compared to simply increasing the dose of a single monotherapy agent. While doubling the dose of a monotherapy typically yields only an additional 1-2.6 mmHg systolic blood pressure reduction, adding a second agent from a different class provides a substantial additional 9 mmHg SBP and 5 mmHg diastolic blood pressure reduction (54) almost doubling the response of monotherapy. This approach also leverages different mechanisms of action, often leading to improved efficacy and a reduced incidence of dose-dependent side effects, as highlighted by trials like ACCOMPLISH (55) and meta-analyses (54,56).
