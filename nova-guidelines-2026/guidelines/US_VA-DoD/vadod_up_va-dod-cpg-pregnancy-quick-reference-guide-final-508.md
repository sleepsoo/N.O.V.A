### **Recommendations**

The evidence-based clinical practice recommendations listed in Table 1 were developed using a systematic approach considering four domains as per the GRADE approach (see Summary of Guideline Development Methodology in the full CPG). These domains include confidence in the quality of the evidence, balance of desirable and undesirable outcomes (i.e., benefits and harms), patient values and preferences, and other implications (e.g., resource use, equity, acceptability).
