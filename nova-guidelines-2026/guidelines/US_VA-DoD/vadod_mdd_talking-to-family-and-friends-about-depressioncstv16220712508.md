4. Share your understanding of depression.

Sample Overview: _Depression is a condition that affects how I feel, act and think. It is different than a feeling of sadness and includes a major change in mood and behavior. This major change in mood and behavior can last for weeks or months. It can include low or irritable mood and a loss of interest or pleasure in usual activities. It interferes with normal functioning and often includes physical symptoms. Depression is treatable, usually using medications and/or psychotherapy._

My Overview:

Please refer to Depression: Facts for Families and talk to your mental health provider about additional information to share with your family.
